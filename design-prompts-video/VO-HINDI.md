# Hindi voiceover — 15-design-prompts-9x16

Hinglish, not pure Hindi: brief, punch list, prompts, stage all stay English
because that is the working vocabulary of the audience. The video is silent, so
this is recorded over it.

This is written for the **20-second flash cut** (prompt card hidden, `PER 0.85`),
not the 54-second version. For the 54 s cut, use the per-topic lines in
`CAPTION.md` and translate them one to one — there is time for all fifteen there.

## Alt hooks, in the order worth testing

1. 15 prompts — design project के हर stage के लिए एक।  *(clearest)*
2. Brief से punch list तक — 15 prompts।  *(fastest, buys a second)*
3. पहली client call से final walkthrough तक — सब कुछ।  *(most curiosity, least clarity)*

## Notes

- **Keep the middle silent.** Fifteen English titles flashing past while Hindi
  is spoken over them puts two languages in the same second and reads as noise.
  The glyphs carry that stretch on their own.
- **`भेज दूँगा` is masculine.** A woman recording this says **भेज दूँगी**
  (*bhej doongi*). Written masculine only because the speaker is unknown.
- On-screen text stays English — see the same note in `../eyes-video/VO-HINDI.md`
  for what burning Devanagari into the frame would actually take.
